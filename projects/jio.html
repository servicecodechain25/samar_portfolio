CONCEPT :-

The visual positions Bigg Buzz as the ultimate gateway into everything that escapes from the Bigg Boss house—every secret, every spark, and every moment of drama that fans crave. By placing the hosts in a vibrant, exaggerated world filled with oversized props like walkie-talkies, magnifying glasses, buzzers and quirky "drama detectors," the design brings alive the idea that Bigg Buzz is where all the hidden action gets caught, decoded and revealed. The playful chaos of bursting elements, bold colours and animated reactions creates an energetic atmosphere that reflects the show's humour, excitement and unpredictability. This creative direction reinforces Bigg Buzz as India's go-to destination for fresh updates, hot takes and unfiltered behind-the-scenes chatter - delivered instantly and exclusively on JioCinema.
